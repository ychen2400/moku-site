Terms of Use

The service

Moku is a study timer and auto-lock tool that helps you reduce distractions. It is not a parental control or device management tool, and it cannot guarantee that other apps will be fully blocked on every phone and system version.

Emergencies

Phone calls and emergency SOS are never blocked. A normal lock can be lifted with an emergency unlock after a wait. Strict mode allows at most 5 unlocks a day; once they are used up, only phone calls and emergency SOS remain available for the rest of that day. Do not rely on the lock feature in situations where you need your phone at all times (for example, while driving or caring for others).

Moku Pro

Subscriptions are paid through the App Store or Google Play and renew automatically. The next period is charged 24 hours before the current one ends. You can cancel at any time on the store's subscriptions page, and you can keep using Pro for the rest of the period you have already paid for. Refunds follow each store's policies. When you change phones, you can restore your purchase with the same store account.

Your data

Your data is stored only on your phone, and we cannot recover it for you. Please check your phone's system backup settings yourself.
