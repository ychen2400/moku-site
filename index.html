支援

Moku 是讀書計時工具：按下科目就開始，時間到、到圖書館、該睡了都會自動擋住讓你分心的 App。也有 Apple Watch 和 Wear OS 版。

上鎖了，怎麼解開？

一般模式：在 Moku 的上鎖畫面按住「長按提早結束」。被擋的 App 畫面上也有「緊急解鎖」，等一下就能解開。電話和緊急求助永遠能用。

嚴格模式解不開？

嚴格模式（Pro）只能緊急解鎖，要等待：第一次 1 分鐘、之後越來越久，一天最多 5 次。這是你自己開啟的設定，沒在上鎖時可以到設定裡關掉。

手錶按開始，手機沒有上鎖？

手錶要連得到手機（藍牙開著、在附近）。連不到時手錶會改成只在手錶計時，讀的時間之後會記到手機。

換手機、重裝後恢復購買

在 Moku 的設定裡按「恢復購買」，用同一個 Apple ID 或 Google 帳號。

取消訂閱

iPhone：設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱 → Moku。Android：Google Play → 付款和訂閱 → 訂閱。

Support

Moku is a study timer: tap a subject to start, and Moku blocks distracting apps on a schedule, at the library, or at bedtime. There are Apple Watch and Wear OS versions too.

How do I unlock?

Normal mode: on the Moku lock screen, press and hold "Hold to end early". The shield on a blocked app also has "Emergency unlock", which works after a short wait. Calls and emergency SOS always work.

I can't unlock in strict mode

Strict mode (Pro) only allows emergency unlocks with a wait: 1 minute the first time, longer after that, up to 5 a day. You turned it on yourself and can turn it off in settings when nothing is locked.

I started on my watch but my phone didn't lock

Your watch needs to reach your phone (nearby, Bluetooth on). When it can't, the watch times the session on its own and saves it to your phone later.

Restore purchases

In Moku's settings, tap "Restore purchases", using the same Apple ID or Google account.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Moku. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
