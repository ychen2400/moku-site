隱私權政策

一句話說明

Moku 沒有帳號，也沒有伺服器。你的資料只存在這支手機裡，我們收不到，也看不到。

存在手機裡的資料

科目、待辦、讀書紀錄、行事曆事件、D-Day、上鎖規則與白名單。這些資料只在手機本機使用。手機系統的備份（iCloud 或 Google）可能會包含它們，由你的系統設定決定。

使用情況存取權（Android）

用來判斷目前開的是哪個 App，才知道該不該上鎖。只在手機上比對，不會記錄或上傳。

螢幕使用時間（iOS）

用來遮蔽白名單以外的 App。依照 Apple 的設計，Moku 看不到你手機裡裝了什麼，也看不到你選了哪些 App。

位置

只有設定地點上鎖時才會用到，用來判斷你是否到達該地點。位置只在手機上比對，不會上傳。

行事曆

用來顯示你原本的行程、排讀書時避開衝突，並把安排寫進專用的「Moku」日曆。行事曆內容不會離開你的手機。

購買

付款完全由 App Store 或 Google Play 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料，只會向商店確認你是否擁有 Moku Pro。

手錶

Apple Watch 與 Wear OS 版只在你的手機和手錶之間傳資料（今天的待辦、讀書時間、計時狀態），透過系統的配對連線，不經過我們的伺服器。

第三方

Moku 沒有廣告，也沒有分析或追蹤工具。

刪除資料

解除安裝 App 就會刪除所有資料。寫進系統行事曆的事件，可以連同「Moku」日曆一起刪除。

Privacy Policy

The short version

Moku has no accounts and no servers. Your data is stored only on this phone. We never receive it and cannot see it.

Data stored on your phone

Subjects, to-dos, study records, calendar events, D-Days, lock rules and allowed apps. This data is used only locally on your phone. Your phone's system backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, depending on your system settings.

Usage access (Android)

Used to tell which app is currently open, so Moku knows whether to lock it. This check happens only on your phone and is never recorded or uploaded.

Screen Time (iOS)

Used to shield apps that are not on your allowed list. By Apple's design, Moku cannot see which apps are installed on your phone or which apps you selected.

Location

Used only when you set up a location lock, to tell whether you have arrived at that place. Your location is checked only on your phone and is never uploaded.

Calendar

Used to show your existing events, avoid conflicts when scheduling study time, and write your plans to a dedicated "Moku" calendar. Your calendar data never leaves your phone.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We only check with the store whether you own Moku Pro.

Watch

The Apple Watch and Wear OS apps only exchange data between your phone and your watch (today's to-dos, study time and timer status) over the system's paired connection. It never goes through our servers.

Third parties

Moku has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. Events written to your system calendar can be deleted together with the "Moku" calendar.
